- **Onset and isolation explainer.** Recomputed the answer onset under both directions (whole, own part) and three rules for the 4B questions with saved profiles. The whole-direction onset rejects the same four answer echoes that isolation rejects; the two disagree only on two letter questions (Mount Fuji, Johns Hopkins). Also found that the answer-echo questions (York's and Mozart's country) still follow the swap 4 of 4, so "answer echo" is a cautious label, and that removing Toronto makes the model fall back to "UK", evidence against a York University → Canada shortcut.

- **Bridge under conformity (narrow project, `conformity` branch).** 25 letter questions on Qwen3.6-27B and 24 on Qwen3.5-4B, alone and with 1–3 agreeing or disagreeing peers, under a neutral and the pilot's deferential wording (706 prompts). Agreeing peers make the bridge stop mattering while the answer stays right, and the bridge stays readable (present but not needed). When the model holds against wrong peers, the bridge holds it. When it gives in, the bridge still pulls in about half of the 27B cases and is not needed in some. The neutral wording gave few give-ins, so the deferential wording was added during the run.
- **Conformity follow-up: copy or backup route (`conformity` branch).** Where removing the bridge did nothing in agree prompts, is the answer coming only from copying, or does the bridge still feed it? Removing the peers' letter direction at their tokens didn't block copying (on the 27B give-ins it moved the balance no more than an everyday word), so it was stopped after 111 prompts. Instead, the swap test (fixed rules in `docs/swap_test_rules.md`): on the 27B, swapping the bridge still flips the answer in 17 of 20 "not needed" prompts (neutral) and 17 of 31 (deferential), so the bridge is a backup route there; on the 4B with the deferential wording it flips 4 of 43, so there the bridge is present but not used. With the peers' letters hidden, the bridge is needed again almost as much as alone. Report: `docs/conformity/routes.html`.
