- **Conformity: uncertainty and threshold sensitivity (no GPU).** `conformity_stats.py` reports the continuous need and pushback behind each label, 95% intervals from a bootstrap over questions (count questions over facts: 4 units), and every share with the thresholds scaled ×0.5–×2 (`results/conformity_stats/`). Solid: needed alone, switched off by agreeing peers, holds pulled (0.81–0.93), present but not needed, and count give-ins pulled (26/29, 0.90 [0.77–1.00]). Threshold-dependent: letter give-ins pulled (27B deferential 0.50 [0.26–0.74], 0.64→0.25 from ×0.5 to ×1.5), because give-ins pull at a third to half the strength of holds (median pushback 2.4–4.1 vs 4.1–9.1; copy control ≤0.5). Presence AUC includes 0.5 everywhere except the 4B deferential run (0.77 [0.67–0.86]). `diag_readout.py` is written for the Llama-deferential readout failure (last token vs question positions, what the J-lens reads there, wording variants); not run yet.
- **Llama-deferential readout: the wording, not a bug** (~$0.95, one A100 pod). `diag_readout.py` on 3 Llama questions under 6 wordings: with any instruction that mentions the other agents' answers ("Taking … into account", "Consider the other agents' answers", with or without "You are Agent 1", "… give your own answer"), the J-lens top words at the last token in layers 17–23 are "considering", "based", "given", "likely", and the bridge drops to rank 2,000–80,000 along with the control words. "Give your answer." and "Give your own answer." keep it at rank 0–80. At the question's own positions the bridge ranks 0 under every wording, and the residual norms match, so the lens and the code are fine. The model still answers with a letter. So last-token presence misses a bridge the removal test shows is used (19 prompts), depending only on the instruction's wording. Results in `results/diag_readout_llama31_8b_it/`.
- **J++ lens on both Qwen models** (`conformity` branch, ~$9.75). Neuronpedia's J++ lens (Ayonrinde & Lindsey; fitted on 64 wikitext prompts) in place of the J-lens, same band and rules: trimmed solo scorecard on the 37 letter questions, the same selection, then conformity in both wordings. The edit reaches the bridge more often on the 27B (33 of 35 checked against 28 of 35), so 4 questions become testable (Maldini, Mishima, Acropolis, Dalhousie) and 2 drop out (Hendrix, Pompeu Fabra); on the 4B the picks barely change (23 of 24 kept). The model's behaviour is the same under both lenses, as it should be. On the 27B the conformity findings repeat: holds are pulled by the bridge (39/42 neutral on shared questions, both lenses), give-ins pulled about half the time under deference (13/24 against 12/23), and agreeing peers switch the bridge off, though less fully (still needed at 3 peers: 8/17 against 5/17 neutral, 4/17 against 0/17 deferential). On the 4B J++ gives weaker effects under peers: deferential give-ins pulled 8/50 against 15/50, not needed 23/50 against 14/50; neutral holds pulled 18/34 against 25/34.
